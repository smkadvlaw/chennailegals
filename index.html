Frequently Asked Questions

What is Anticipatory Bail?
Anticipatory bail is a legal provision that allows a person to seek bail in anticipation of an arrest for a non-bailable offense. It is filed under Section 438 of the Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC).
How long does a mutual consent divorce take?
A mutual consent divorce typically takes between 6 to 18 months, depending on the mandatory cooling-off period and the court's schedule.
What are the grounds for a Writ Petition?
Writ petitions can be filed in High Courts (Article 226) or the Supreme Court (Article 32) when fundamental rights are violated or when an authority fails to perform its legal duty.
What is the role of a Muttawali in Waqf property?
A Muttawali is a manager or custodian of a Waqf property. They are responsible for the administration and ensuring the property is used for the intended charitable or religious purposes according to the Waqf deed.
What should I do if I receive a Charge Memo from my employer?
You should immediately seek legal advice to draft a precise and factual explanation. Failure to respond adequately can lead to disciplinary action or termination.
